Slide 6 — NLP Models Used

1. Logistic Regression (Baseline)

Simple, fast, strong performance with TF-IDF.

2. sep-CNN (Sentence-piece CNN)

Convolutional model for text with subword tokens.

Good for short phrases referencing datasets.

3. RNN-based Models

BiLSTM — reads text both forward & backward.

GRU — memory-efficient RNN variant.
